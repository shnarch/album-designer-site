Plain text, one line per row, if your voice tool wants it pasted:

Step one: add your photos. With a Claude key, the app analyzes every photo you add. It finds the main subject of each photo. It removes near-duplicates, and guides the auto design.

Step two: split into stories. It's optional. Stories help the auto design. Each story gets its own pages, and can be redesigned without touching the other pages. Split by time: for example, a story per month for a year book. Or split by hand: click between two photos. Tag photos as Favorite, Alternative, or Keep together, to guide the design. No stories? That works too.

Step three: lay out the album. Every story gets its pages, spread by spread.

Step four: redesign with a click. You can redesign a page with a click, a spread, and even a whole story. Keep redesigning until you find a layout you like. Photo orientation counts in the design, and the analysis keeps your main subject from being cropped.

Step five: export print-ready files. Export print-ready files to be used with whichever print provider you choose.

That's an album in ten minutes. For more control, see Professional design.
